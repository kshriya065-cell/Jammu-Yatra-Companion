DISCOVER CULTURE & TASTE

Cultural Foods & History

Read and listen to the descriptions of famous foods and heritage.

loading="lazy">

Rajma Chawal

A much-loved traditional meal of red kidney beans cooked in thick gravy with whole spices, usually served with steamed rice. It holds immense cultural significance in Jammu.

Kalari Kulcha

Jammu's famous traditional ripened cheese, known as Kalari. It is often pan-fried and served stuffed inside a bun (kulcha) with tangy tamarind and spicy mint chutney.

kachalu chaat

A tangy local snack made from colocasia and mixed with spices and sour flavour.A great choice for visitor who want to explore the region's street-food culture.

Bahu fort

A historic fort situated on the banks of Tawi river. It was built over 3,000 years ago by Raja Bahulochan. The fort houses a temple dedicated to the Hindu goddess Kali, known locally as Bawe Wali Mata.

Raghunath Temple

One of the largest temple complexes in northern India. Built by Maharaja Gulab Singh and his son Maharaja Ranbir Singh in the mid-19th century, it has seven shrines with gold-plated spires.

Dogra Heritage

The Dogra dynasty formed the erstwhile Kingdom of Jammu and Kashmir. Their vibrant culture, traditional art forms like Basohli paintings, and martial history remain central to the region's identity.
